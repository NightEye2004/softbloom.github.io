# 🌸 ABOUT SOFTBLOOM

SoftBloom is a Minecraft community built for players who enjoy survival, adventure, creativity, and friendly multiplayer gameplay.

Our Discord server is the main hub for:
• Announcements
• Server updates
• Support & guides
• Whitelist access
• Community events
• Shop purchases
• Player interaction

Whether you enjoy classic survival, enhanced SMP progression, or modded RPG adventures, SoftBloom has a server for you.

**Welcome to SoftBloom — play, survive, and bloom with us.** 🌸
